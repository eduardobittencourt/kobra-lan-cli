# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in issues, reviews, discussions and other project spaces
must treat others with respect. Welcome different experience levels, give
constructive technical feedback, respect privacy and accept good-faith corrections.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, doxxing and deliberate disclosure of private information are unacceptable.
Disagreement about technical choices does not justify abusive conduct.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact maintainer Eduardo Bittencourt privately at
[eduardo@bittencourt.dev](mailto:eduardo@bittencourt.dev) with conduct concerns.
Share only information needed to assess the incident. Reports are handled
privately to the extent possible; absolute confidentiality is not promised.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, request a correction, issue a
warning, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation according to severity
and repetition. Enforcement considers context and impact; retaliation against
reporters is unacceptable. The same standards apply to maintainers.

For vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than public reports.
